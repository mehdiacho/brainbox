# History

Repositories folded into this one. Each keeps its full commit history under
`history/<name>/`. To read those commits, find the "Fold <name>" commit and run
`git log <that commit>^2`. The original repositories were deleted on 2026-11-14; everything they held is in this one.

- **IdeaSpace** (2026-10-18 to 2026-10-18): `history/IdeaSpace/`. The original repository `IdeaSpace` was deleted on 2026-11-14 once its history was here.
- **Idea-Pad** (2024-07-13 to 2024-07-13): `history/Idea-Pad/`. The original repository `Idea-Pad` was deleted on 2026-11-14 once its history was here.
